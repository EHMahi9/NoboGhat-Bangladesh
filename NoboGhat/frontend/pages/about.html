About NoboGhat

Digitalizing Bangladesh's Inland Waterways

Our Vision

To become Bangladesh's trusted digital platform for inland water logistics and shared cargo transport.

The Challenge

The Problem We Solve

Moving goods on Bangladesh's rivers has long been slow and fragmented. Here is what we're changing.

Fragmented Communication

Cargo owners and boat captains depend on phone calls and word of mouth. There is no central place to find a boat, compare options, or plan a shipment.

Reliance on Brokers

Middlemen control routes and pricing, inflating costs for farmers and small traders while leaving boat owners with unpredictable income.

Underutilized Boats

Vessels sail with empty cargo holds because capacity is invisible. Better matching means fuller boats and lower per-kg costs for everyone.

Our Community

Who We Serve

Farmers

Farmers seek affordable transport and can now share cargo space for small agricultural harvests instead of paying for an entire boat.
